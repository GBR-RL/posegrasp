## Grasp success

A grasp planned from the pose estimate, judged on the true pose (see the protocol). Failures: no pose, no feasible grasp in the scene, collision with the object or the scene, fingers closing on nothing, or contacts outside the friction cone.

Grasps are ranked by their robustness to pose errors; the *nominal* column ranks them ignoring pose errors (the ablation).
